# Profile mascot artwork

The hero uses `page-mascot` with two locally served, transparent 768×768 WebP atlases in `public/mascots/`. Each atlas has nine equal cells with a 16-pixel transparent inset. The artwork was generated with the built-in imagegen tool from `public/profile-cropped.png` and inspected for head direction, matching appearance, and transparency.

The mascot follows a fine pointer and reacts to clicks, keyboard activation, and touch taps. As the reader scrolls, it keeps its original horizontal alignment beside the name and gradually travels down the viewport, stopping above the bottom edge and returning beside the name at the top of the page. It is 48 pixels on phones and 72 pixels on larger screens. The page retains its original width and padding; the floating mascot may overlap content. Its original slot stays reserved to prevent layout shifts. Scroll movement is disabled when reduced motion is preferred, and the package respects reduced motion for its click bounce. It does not use phone orientation sensors.

## Moving the mascot

The photo-to-name movement includes three subtle walking steps: a small vertical bounce and alternating tilt, fading out at each end. The steps follow scroll progress, reverse when scrolling up, and stop when the mascot is dragged. Reduced-motion users do not see this movement.

After 1.5 seconds the greeting also displays a small "Hi! 👋" bubble with a brief hand wave. The bubble disappears after about two seconds. Reduced-motion users see the greeting without the wave animation.

The initial mascot is 24 pixels larger than its usual size (72 pixels on phones and 96 pixels on larger screens). It shrinks to its usual size as it moves beside the name. On arrival it looks up-right, then after 1.5 seconds faces forward and gives a smile reaction. Dragging before the greeting prevents the automatic reaction from interrupting it.

At the top of the page, only the mascot appears in the profile photo's position, with the photo hidden. The profile photo is only rendered on desktop (md and up); on smaller screens it is hidden and the mascot stays in its place beside the name, then continues its existing scroll-following behavior. During the first 120 pixels of scrolling on desktop, it smoothly moves to its original position beside the name while the photo fades into view, then continues its existing scroll-following behavior. Scrolling back to the top reverses this movement. Dragging reveals the photo immediately. Reduced-motion users keep the photo visible and the mascot beside the name without this movement.

Drag the mascot with a mouse or touch to place it anywhere inside the viewport. After a drag, it stays where you put it while scrolling and is kept within the screen when resized. Moving less than six pixels is treated as a tap, so ordinary clicks and taps still trigger expressions. Dragging does not trigger a click reaction. Keyboard users can focus the mascot and move it with arrow keys (Shift moves faster); Home restores its automatic scroll position. Dragging does not change page width or spacing.

Preserve swept wavy black hair, medium warm skin, short moustache and chin beard, small red tika on forehead, pale blue polo shirt with no logo. NOT photorealistic. EXACT 3 by 3 equal grid, nine isolated head-and-shoulder portraits, no grid lines, text, shadows or background. Head 1.5 times shoulder width. Identical fixed front-facing shoulders and torso, identical scale, center position and baseline in every cell; only head turns. Each bust fits within central 75% of its cell with generous transparent margins on every side. Direction order from viewer perspective: row1 looking up-left, up, up-right; row2 looking left, straight ahead, right; row3 looking down-left, down, down-right. Head visibly turns toward those directions, not only pupils. Left means viewer's left. Calm friendly smile in every cell. All nine cells precisely aligned for CSS background-size 300% 300%. Deliver actual alpha transparency. Save generated asset to project if possible; return local file path metadata.
